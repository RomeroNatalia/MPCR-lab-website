The MPCR Labs Round Table Series presents **AI in Education: Central Tensions** — a guided round table debate exploring the unresolved tensions at the intersection of artificial intelligence and education.

**Event Details:**

- **Date:** Tuesday, April 7, 2026
- **Time:** 5:15 PM Reception | 6:00 PM Kickoff
- **Location:** The Gruber Sandbox, FAU Boca Raton Campus

**Central Tensions:**

- Cognitive offloading vs. deep learning
- Personalization vs. belonging
- Assistance vs. authorship
- Innovation vs. governance

Artificial intelligence is rapidly entering classrooms, institutions, and policy frameworks — but not without unresolved tensions. This guided round table session brings together educators, policy-makers, and researchers in AI, education, and learning sciences for a structured, evidence-based debate on where AI is supporting learning — and where it may be quietly undermining it.

Rather than aiming for consensus, the session is designed to surface competing perspectives, tradeoffs, and open questions.
