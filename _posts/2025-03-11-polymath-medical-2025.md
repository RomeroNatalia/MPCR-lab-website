The Gruber AI Sandbox hosted Polymath Medical 2025 — an evening bringing together medical professionals, researchers, and students to explore how AI and new computational paradigms will transform healthcare.

The event featured Michael Levin (Tufts University) presenting his work on bioelectricity and cellular intelligence for cancer and regeneration, alongside panel speakers Dan Elton (NHGRI), Will Hahn (FAU), and Gil Blander (InsideTracker), moderated by Curt Jaimungal (Theories of Everything).

Discussion topics ranged from AI-powered hospitals and the evolving role of physicians to complex systems frameworks for medicine and practical interventions in human health and aging. The event opened with remarks from Rubin Gruber.
