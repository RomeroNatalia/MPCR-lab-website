MPCR Lab co-founders Prof. William Hahn and Prof. Elan Barenholtz discussed synesthesia, digital psychedelics, pseudo-psychedelics, infohazards, psychological prompt engineering, and Solresol in an Ekkolapto Polymath Salon. Hahn presented his theory of "psychotokenomics" — how unorthodox forms of language modify machine and biological intelligence.
