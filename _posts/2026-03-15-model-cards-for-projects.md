title: Model Cards Now Available on Project Pages
---

MPCR Lab project pages now support **Model Cards** -- structured documentation that provides a clear, standardized overview of each project's technical details. This addition reflects our commitment to transparency and reproducibility in research.

Each Model Card is organized into tabbed sections covering architecture, training data, evaluation results, known limitations, and reproducibility instructions. The goal is to give readers -- whether they are collaborators, reviewers, or students -- a concise and honest account of what a model does, how it was built, and where its boundaries lie.

The concept of model cards originated in the machine learning community as a response to the growing need for accountability in AI systems. By adopting this practice at the lab level, we aim to build good documentation habits early, especially for students who will carry these standards into their future careers.

Model Cards are optional but encouraged for all active projects. They can be created and edited directly through the lab's CRM, and changes sync automatically to the public website. Project leads are invited to begin filling in cards for their current work.

We believe that open science is not just about sharing code and data -- it is about making the reasoning behind research decisions visible. Model Cards are one step in that direction.
