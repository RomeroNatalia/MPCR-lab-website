title: Model Cards Now Available on Project Pages
---

Project pages on the MPCR website now include **Model Cards** — tabbed documentation sections covering architecture, data, results, limitations, and reproducibility for each project.

Model Cards are edited through the CRM and sync automatically to the website. They are optional but project leads are encouraged to fill them in as their work develops.
