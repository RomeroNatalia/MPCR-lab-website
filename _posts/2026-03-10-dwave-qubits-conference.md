MPCR Lab participated in the D-Wave Qubits 2026 Conference, engaging in strategic discussions with leaders advancing real-world quantum computing.

Our team connected with key figures including Allison Schwartz, Global Government Relations and Public Affairs Leader at D-Wave Quantum Inc.; Catherine McGeoch, Principal Scientist and Benchmarking Lead; Sara Jamous, Senior Technical Content Developer and Instructor at D-Wave; and Kathleen Amm from the National High Magnetic Field Laboratory.

These conversations are part of our ongoing work with FAU's D-Wave Advantage2 quantum computer — a 4,400+ qubit annealing system being deployed on the Boca Raton campus for research in optimization, materials simulation, and AI.
