title: Announcing the MPCR Digital Poster Gallery
---

We are pleased to announce the launch of the **MPCR Digital Poster Gallery**, a new platform for lab members to showcase their research in a rich, accessible format. The gallery is now live on our website and open for submissions.

Each poster entry supports a full abstract, a high-resolution poster image, an optional video walkthrough, and direct links to the associated project page. This means that work presented at conferences, symposia, or internal lab sessions can live on as a permanent, citable part of the lab's public record. Visitors can browse posters by project, semester, or research area.

The gallery was designed to lower the barrier between producing research and sharing it. Rather than letting posters collect dust after a single presentation, lab members can now make their work discoverable to collaborators, prospective students, and the broader research community.

We are currently accepting poster submissions for the **Spring 2026 session**. If you have presented or plan to present research this semester, we encourage you to prepare your entry. Submissions can be coordinated through the lab's internal CRM system.

For questions about the submission process, reach out to your project lead or contact the lab directors. We look forward to seeing the gallery grow with the work being done across our 20 active projects.
