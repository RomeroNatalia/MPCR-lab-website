title: 'Lab Infrastructure Update: New Tools for MPCR'

Over the past several weeks, the MPCR Lab has rolled out a series of infrastructure improvements designed to make project management, member coordination, and public communication more efficient.

At the center of these changes is a new **internal CRM system** built for the lab's specific needs. The CRM handles project tracking, member profiles, poster submissions, and documentation -- all in one place. Lab directors and project leads can manage their teams, update project details, and monitor progress without juggling spreadsheets or scattered documents.

A key feature is **automated website synchronization**. When project information or member profiles are updated in the CRM, those changes propagate to the public website at [mpcrlab.com](https://mpcrlab.com) automatically. This eliminates the manual overhead of keeping two systems in sync and ensures that the website always reflects the current state of the lab.

We have also improved **member profiles** on the website, giving each researcher a dedicated page with their bio, project affiliations, and links to their work. For prospective members, a new **join form** on the website provides a straightforward path to express interest in the lab. Applications are reviewed by the directors and, once accepted, new members are onboarded through a guided process.

These tools are designed to stay out of the way and let researchers focus on what matters: the science. Questions or feedback can be directed to the lab directors.
