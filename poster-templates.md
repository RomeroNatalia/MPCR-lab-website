<div class="uk-margin-large-top">
  <h2>How to Use</h2>
  <div class="uk-grid-small uk-child-width-1-3@m" data-uk-grid>
    <div>
      <div class="uk-card uk-card-default uk-card-body uk-card-small">
        <h4>1. Copy a Template</h4>
        <p>Copy one of the example poster <code>.md</code> files from the <code>_posters/</code> directory and rename it for your project.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div>
      <div class="uk-card uk-card-default uk-card-body uk-card-small">
        <h4>2. Edit Frontmatter</h4>
        <p>Update the YAML frontmatter with your title, authors, sections, images, and references. Content uses Markdown.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div>
      <div class="uk-card uk-card-default uk-card-body uk-card-small">
        <h4>3. Print or Export</h4>
        <p>Open the poster page in your browser and use <strong>Print / Save PDF</strong>. Set paper size to match your poster dimensions.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="uk-margin-large-top">
  <h2>Frontmatter Reference</h2>
  <div class="uk-overflow-auto">
    <table class="uk-table uk-table-striped uk-table-small">
      <thead>
        <tr>
          <th>Field</th>
          <th>Type</th>
          <th>Description</th>
        </tr>
      </thead>
      <tbody>
        <tr><td><code>layout</code></td><td>string</td><td>Must be <code>poster-template</code></td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>title</code></td><td>string</td><td>Poster title (appears in header)</td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>orientation</code></td><td>string</td><td><code>horizontal</code> (48x36) or <code>vertical</code> (36x48)</td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>presenters</code></td><td>list</td><td>Each item has <code>name</code> and optional <code>affiliation</code> superscript</td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>advisor</code></td><td>string</td><td>Faculty advisor name</td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>department</code></td><td>string</td><td>Defaults to MPCR Lab</td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>institution</code></td><td>string</td><td>Defaults to Florida Atlantic University</td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>contact_email</code></td><td>string</td><td>Contact email for footer</td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>sections</code></td><td>list</td><td>Each has <code>title</code>, <code>content</code> (Markdown), optional <code>accent</code>, <code>full_width</code>, <code>image</code>, <code>image_caption</code></td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>images</code></td><td>list</td><td>Standalone figures: <code>title</code>, <code>src</code>, <code>caption</code>, <code>placeholder</code></td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>acknowledgments</code></td><td>string</td><td>Acknowledgments bar text</td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>qr_code</code></td><td>string</td><td>Path to QR code image</td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>project_url</code></td><td>string</td><td>URL linked from QR code area</td></tr>
        <tr><td><code>logo</code></td><td>string</td><td>Custom logo path (defaults to MPCR-White.svg)</td></tr>
      </tbody>
    </table>
  </div>
</div>
